Privacy, with a smile.

Effective October 16, 2026. SmileGate is developed by Beau Sterling.

Your camera and face

With your permission, the front camera and Apple ARKit estimate smile movements entirely on your iPhone. Camera images, face geometry and expression scores are used transiently to run the countdown. SmileGate does not save them, upload them, share them, recognize your identity, build a face profile, or use them for advertising or training. They are discarded as the camera session runs and ends. There is no microphone recording. The optional laughter bonus is self-reported.

What stays on your device

The app stores your settings, private Screen Time selection tokens supplied by Apple, session and smile counters, unlock expiry times, and onboarding state. App Group storage shares the information needed to enforce your choices between the app and its Screen Time extensions. Your selected app names and usage are not sent to the developer.

Purchases

Apple processes optional App Store purchases and subscription billing. SmileGate checks verified Apple transactions on your device to determine Pro access. The developer does not receive your payment card information. Apple handles purchase data under its own privacy policy.

No tracking

SmileGate includes no developer analytics, advertising SDKs, account registration, or tracking. The developer does not operate a server that receives your app data. Apple may independently handle App Store, operating system and diagnostic information according to its settings and privacy policies.

Your controls and deletion

You can use a breathing pause without the camera, revoke camera or Screen Time permission in iPhone Settings, or turn pauses off in the app. Settings → Delete local data stops monitoring and removes your app selection, preferences and local counters. Deleting local data does not cancel an App Store subscription; manage subscriptions through your Apple Account. Your device may retain app data through system backups under your own backup settings.

Support and this website

This static support site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process request metadata under its privacy policy. Opening a support issue shares the information you choose to include with GitHub and the public. Do not include sensitive personal information.

Contact

For privacy questions or assistance, contact Beau Sterling through a support request. The current policy will always be available at this URL.
