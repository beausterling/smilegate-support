Smile App adds a small smile or breathing pause before you return to distracting apps. Choose your apps, set your boundaries, and give yourself a moment.

Privacy policyContact support

Getting started

Connect Screen Time and choose one individual app for free. Hold a comfortable smile for 5, 10 or 15 seconds, or use the camera-free breathing alternative. Each completed unlock grants 16 minutes of access. Optional session allowances, cooldowns and scheduled breaks help you stop and reset.

A pause isn’t opening

On iOS 26.5 or later, the shield’s primary button opens Smile App. On earlier versions, tap the unlock notification or open Smile App from your Home Screen. Check that notifications are allowed if you use the notification option.

Camera help

Use a compatible iPhone, allow camera access in Settings, and keep your face clearly visible. The countdown resets when you stop smiling or tracking is lost. You can always use the breathing option if the camera is unavailable or smiling is uncomfortable.

You stay in control

Turn pauses off from the dashboard. To remove your settings, app selection and counters, open Settings → Delete local data. You can revoke Screen Time authorization in the iPhone’s Settings app.

Purchases

One app is free. Optional Pro purchases unlock multiple apps, categories and websites. The app shows current localized App Store prices before purchase. Restore a previous purchase from the Pro screen. Manage or cancel a yearly subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Need help?

Open a support request with your iPhone model, iOS version, and a description of what happened. Requests are public: please do not include passwords, private app selections, camera images, or other sensitive information.

Smile App is a self-control tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or measure mental health.
